The new Avatar project is here, and no, I'm not talking about blue people Avatar, but about beloved story of The Last Airbender. I am a full-time Avatar: The Last Airbender (later I will refer to it just as ATLA) fan. I rewatch the original show at least once a year. Every time something bad happens, or just life takes me on the ride for fun or death I rewatch it. The show not only gives me a great comfort, but unlike the opinion of Netflix ATLA is not a show only for kids. There is so much wisdom in every and each episode, that it is truly a gold mine in self improving. 

So, you can imagine how very excited I was to hear there is a movie coming out from my favourite universe, and not only that, but a movie about grown up Avatar team. My goodness, it was truly a second Christmas for me. But movie has come and go, and my feelings about this project are very mixed. 

I watched it maybe a week after its release, and I wasn't so well versed in controversies and the leaks that happened. So, I truly went into it with zero expectations.

*!!!BEWARE SPOILERS FOR AVATAR AANG MOVIE!!!*
## My review - what was bad and what good
Well. First, I loved the vibe so much. More leaning into Indian culture helped so much. I loved all the characters and how the movie was structured. Overall, it was a fun ride. I had an opportunity to see my favourite characters meet again and do fun adventure. 

But. A huge BUT really. 

It is so messy. I have a slight beef with art style, because I understand they leaned more into an anime style, and I'm truly not undermining the hard work of all the animators that worked on this project. Movie is beautiful, but I felt that a lot of characters lost their personality. Their shape, noses deleted essentially and so on just did a number on them. But hey, that's a very teeny tinny issue that is totally just my problem. BUT. The characters and the whole plot line is just all over the place.
