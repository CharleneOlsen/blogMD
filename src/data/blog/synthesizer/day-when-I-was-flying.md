Today I had an opportunity to fly. It was very spontaneous as my father told me in the morning, that I'm going to fly. 



I've met with the pilot, who is a truly such an inspiring and fuul soul. I don't want to encover a lot about his personal life, but for the sake of my argument. He was an IT guy managing projects and he was running his company. Some may say that this is a dream life, and I'm sure he said the same about himself. But, and you can see this trend in lead engineers in IT industry, he quit. He sold his company and decided to just live. 

He bought a house in the middle of nowhere, well, for us city people it is nowhere, but for him, its his land in the middle of a forest. He is living very sufficiently and self sustaiably. He has solar panel, small wind turbine. He self-holsts almost everything. He decided that he wants to enjoy life. 

I asked him then how he got to flying. He told me that he once trained to be a pilot of those big comertial airplanes, but life turned him the other way. But then, his girlfriend, who is a such a wonderful soul, bought him a jum with a parashoot. Because they came quite early to the hangar, he dcided to try being a pilot for an hour course, and he understood that this is what had been missing in his life. 

Whilst I was talking to him, I asked him, how is he sustaining this livelyhood, becuase for me, such life is so unimaginable. He told me that his life philosophy is that people don't need many things to live. He has a house, a partner, and he is more than happy living a quite life so the need to chase trend, spend a ton of money, on subscribtions, media, technology is useless for him. 

This is tradeoff to his hobby, flying. Becuase this is him main expenditure and he is more than happy to make this sacrifice. 

He said that the flying is an ultimate freedom, also becuase there are no rules. He can fly anywhere anytime. He doesnt have to log his flight anywhere, because he flies in a different zone and also the aircraft is way too small to bother anyone. So, there is no trafic, no people, nobody. Only you and the sky. 

I think there is a lot to learn from this story.
