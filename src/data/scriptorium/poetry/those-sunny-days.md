I was hit with a wave of warm feelings
when I laid on the field of flowers
in the middle of nowhere
it was warm
but not hot
I heard bugs walk around me in a frenzy
I heard birds on a hunt to feed their sprouts
I remember the hot asfalt 
I felt so at peace
the war of the mind had stopped to cherish the moment
oh and the smell
it was all so healing

then the winter came 
the flowers are dead
and will never come back
we have killed ourselves
to hunt for these feeling elsewhere
