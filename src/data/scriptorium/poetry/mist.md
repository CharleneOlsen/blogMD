mist lays heavy
it covers all things
making others uncertain

I don't see the path anymore
I only sense the vague feeling of it

there is beauty in dispersed light
cast by street lamps

I haven't seen the mist in a long time
it's like seeing your best friend
after years of separation

you feel so much 
that you can't describe

words are worthless
yet the mist is still here
