I've recently finished reading Project Hail Mary. Before I started reading the book though I watched the movie, because although I am a big fan of Andy Weir I had absolutely no idea this man published a new book. Yes, you can say I was living under the rock. 

I remember sitting after the movie and finding out that this is a book by Andy Weir, you just had to see my facial expression. Right there I set a goal of reading the book, because I love science and I love science fiction. The best thing about Andy is that he uses correct (mostly) terms to describe technology or phenomena used to explore the world. There are chromatography, microscopes, the way Grace found out what kind of gravity force he is under when he woke up and so on. 

I loved the movie wholeheartedly, but my only issue is the way Grace is as a character compared to the book. Of course book is going to be completely different from the movie due to different storytelling possibilities. But in the book I understood why Stratt chose Grace. Yes, he might have not been the most clever scientist on one particular topic, but he was chosen because of his huge variety in knowledge. Stratt could ask him literally anything, and he would immediately tell her, what that technology is, where is it used and what kind of pros and cons it introduces. He was the polymath really. In the movie, though, I haven't seen that. There Grace is really more of, yes he has the gene to survive the coma so that's why he is here.

But despite that, Project Hail Mary is in the top of my list in both movies and books of this year. So I do recommend watching or read or both, because both media is incredible and will give you hope we all need right now.
