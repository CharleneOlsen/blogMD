If you have no idea what to watch with your morning coffee, lunch, making laundry, or gaming, I wholeheartedly recommend you a channel [Reject Convenience](https://www.youtube.com/@rejectconvenience). This guy is so amazing, if you are loosing hope, as I'm sure we all do when we see the news, watch some of his videos. Some are short, and some are long, but each of them are made with love and care, which you can truly feel from his videos. 

I've recently watched his video about [*How to do anything (without AI)* ](https://youtu.be/R-edYoZZosg?si=lRBmpCJgysxEaUN7)and it opened my eyes on some things I never considered. For example, a different point of view on the whole education system from a person that attended a school with a way different approach to teaching. And I'm really amazed that even with his circumstances he doesn't hate education but on the contrary, he makes educational videos. He shades a light on policies, which nobody really reads. But he does. And it is really beautiful to see such person not dwell on a bad experience but making most of it. I know many people that attended for example Montessori schools that just gave up with educational system, but he didn't and I truly admire that. 

He really makes me hopeful, because he doesn't view the world through gray glasses, where everything is bad and we should all die. He is like the Project Hail Mary book, which shows how humans can be incredibly creative, and have a positive influence on the surrounding world. Yes, there is always going to be someone very bad unfortunately, but then you see these people who are truly a ray of sunshine in a storm.
