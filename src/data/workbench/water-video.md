If you are looking for a video to watch with your meal today, I highly recommend a video by David Achu [the water video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=by4reOwTZh4).

In this video, this beautiful human being, talks about water, its importance for our life, but more specifically he answers a question of what is really classified as a water waste. Because if you have at least some knowledge, you know that water is never wasted, because of water cycle. If I pour water on the ground, that water is not "wasted" because it will eventually evaporate, then gather in the clouds, and manifest in the rain, which will bring that water back to us. 

But David gives a great overview why this is not really the issue we face today, but rather that we waste the water that is available now, which is the most important metric. Most chilling statistics he gives is the by 2050 3 in 4 people will face a water scarcity, and that is truly horrifying. Because we need water for everything. Cooking, drinking, cleaning and much more. 

I would have said to you that the main issue is the AI data centers, which is not entirely wrong, but how David points out, agriculture wasted much more water. Or just for example an irrigation of golf courses wastes more water.

If you want some existential crisis with your food go ahead and watch it. Also, I pretty much recommend any video made by this creator, they are highly educational and very funny even though he talks about serious topics.
